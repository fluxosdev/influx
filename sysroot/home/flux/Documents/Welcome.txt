Welcome to inFlux!

inFlux is a small x86-64 operating system with a preemptive, multithreaded
kernel, a btrfs driver, SATA and NVMe storage drivers, and a desktop that
takes its cues from macOS.

Things to try:
  - Open the Terminal from the Dock and type `help`.
  - Browse the mounted btrfs volumes under /mnt in Finder.
  - Edit this file and press Cmd+S to save it.
  - Open the Task Manager to watch threads being scheduled.
